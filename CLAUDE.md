# watch-picker: agent handoff

A public Pages mirror of the SFW Watch Picker. It exists because its-popcorn-time is private and Pages needs a public repo on this plan.

## Rules
- Don't edit the site files here. Change `docs/` in thiccolasscage/its-popcorn-time. Its `.github/workflows/sync-watch-picker.yml` copies `index.html`, `catalog-with-where.json`, and `mood-packs.json` here and updates the "Copied from" commit in README.md. Commits by `github-actions[bot]` titled "Sync from its-popcorn-time@…" come from that workflow.
- SFW lane only. Keep Theme Spin, the NSFW catalog, and Aisle 69 out of this repo and out of thiccolasscage.github.io's root repo (which hosts Theme Spin).
- No API keys here, ever.
